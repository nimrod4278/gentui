# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Give and receive technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, trolling, and other abusive conduct are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to a maintainer through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants based on the severity and pattern of behavior.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
